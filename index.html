🔭 Research

My research is centred on advancing Language Models (LMs) across two synergistic dimensions: linguistic competence and complex reasoning. On the linguistic side, I investigate controlled text generation and multilingual adaptation, aiming to enhance how models navigate complex stylistic and cross-lingual contexts. Complementing this, my research delves into the thinking and reasoning mechanisms of LMs, focusing on optimising the accuracy and efficiency. Together, my research aims to shape humane AI that integrates precise, context-aware language generation with efficient and reliable reasoning.

Linguistic Competence

  • Controlled Generation: Text Style, Figurative Language
  • Multilinguality: Machine Translation, Knowledge Transfer

Complex Reasoning

  • Efficient Learning: Reinforcement Learning, Curriculum Learning
  • Reliable Reasoning: Reasoning Consistency, Reasoning Robustness
